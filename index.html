01 — About

Part strategist, part maker, entirely people-first.

Pontianak, West Kalimantan · Indonesia

I'm Doni Purnama — one of three Co-Founders of VERDE Creative, a creative agency in Pontianak, Indonesia. My two partners and I built it together and still work side by side, growing the agency as one team. Since 2023, I've handled content trend research, scriptwriting, short-form video editing, graphic design, and client relations for 7+ brands.

Alongside running the agency, I remain active in live wedding event operations as an Event Runner at Sapphire Wedding Organization, building on an earlier foundation in fast-paced retail customer service at Miss Evelyn Cake & Pastry. Balancing both worlds shapes how I work: great creative output only lands when backed by clear communication, composure under pressure, and reliable follow-through.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Digital Business from Sabda Setia Institute of Technology and Business (GPA 3.90/4.00, full scholarship recipient) and work seamlessly in Bahasa Indonesia, English (PTE B2), and Teochew.

02 — My role

THE WHOLE PROCESS.

VERDE Creative manages the accounts. Inside the agency, I own the content process end to end — from the first trend idea to the final feed layout.

01

Trend & idea research

Finding the angle worth making — what the audience cares about right now.

02

Scripting

Writing the hook, the flow and the words that sound like the brand.

03

Shooting

Directing and capturing the footage, on location when the story needs it.

04

Video editing

Cutting, pacing, captions and sound for short-form that holds attention.

05

Feed editing

Designing carousels and grids so the whole feed reads as one brand.

06

Publishing & scheduling

Getting each piece out at the right moment, on every platform.

VERDE Creative manages the accounts. I make the pieces.
